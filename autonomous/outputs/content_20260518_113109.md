# Impact of the Industrial Revolution

The Industrial Revolution, a period of unprecedented technological advancement that began in the late 18th century, fundamentally changed global society. It was a precursor to the First World War in terms of its scale of impact.

Key inventions like the steam engine and the power loom transformed manufacturing and transportation. This led to mass migration from rural areas to cities, fueling urban growth and new social structures. The economic landscape shifted from agrarian to industrial production, creating new wealth but also new social challenges like poor working conditions and child labor.

The societal transformation was immense, leading to new ideologies and class structures, ultimately shaping the modern world.
